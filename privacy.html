Privacy Policy for KharchBook

Last updated: September 2026

KharchBook values your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled within our personal expense tracking application.

1. Information We Collect

• Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we authenticate your identity and retrieve your email address solely to create and secure your sync profile.

• Financial Logs: Transaction titles, amounts, payment source categories, and EMI details entered by you are saved locally in your browser/device and synced to secure Firebase Firestore databases under your private account.

2. Data Security & Storage

Your records are kept private. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties. Cloud synchronization is powered by Google Firebase security protocols.

3. Offline Storage

KharchBook operates offline-first using local storage on your device. You can erase your locally cached data anytime directly from the Settings menu.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us via our developer GitHub profile.
